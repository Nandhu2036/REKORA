Executive Summary

01. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Engineering projects should not end at submission.

Today's student engineering projects involve a highly disconnected set of activities. A typical engineering student must independently navigate project discovery, component sourcing, CAD modification, fabrication, coding, testing, and documentation. Once a project is evaluated, the physical hardware is often discarded, and the engineering knowledge developed during the process is lost.

REKORA connects these fragmented activities into one continuous ecosystem.

Problem & Opportunity

02. THE PROBLEM

The academic engineering lifecycle is economically inefficient and structurally fragmented.

01
Fragmented Resources

Ideation, component sourcing, CAD modification, manufacturing, and documentation exist in isolated silos.

02
Repeated Hardware Purchases

Students frequently purchase expensive components and hardware kits required only temporarily for a single prototype.

03
Limited Access to Fabrication

Moving from digital CAD to a physical prototype is a bottleneck due to limited accessible 3D printing facilities.

04
Loss of Project Knowledge

Once evaluated, troubleshooting steps and design logic disappear. There is no centralized system to capture output.

05
Projects Restart From Zero

Because prior work is inaccessible, students cannot easily build upon previous engineering solutions.

06
Limited Continuity

Subsequent cohorts are forced to build identical basic systems year after year, preventing ambitious engineering evolution.

03. THE OPPORTUNITY

By consolidating the fragmented components of project development into a unified platform, we can reduce the financial barrier to innovation while dramatically improving the quality of student output.

The integration of project discovery, hardware sourcing, and digital repositories opens the door for a circular academic economy—where resources are rented, reused, and continuously iterated upon.

Core Workflows

05. HOW REKORA WORKS

Discovery & AI

Users begin with a problem, budget, or components. The platform algorithmically recommends suitable initiatives, while the AI Project Engineer frames constraints and generates a blueprint.

Component Sourcing

To reduce unnecessary purchases, REKORA integrates project BOMs directly with inventory. Students can choose to Buy, Rent, Use Existing, or Borrow components item by item.

CAD & Fabrication

REKORA connects digital design to the physical build. Students customize CAD models aligned with their requirements, routing them directly to campus 3D printing.

Build & Documentation

During physical assembly, REKORA provides integration logic, troubleshooting, and standardized documentation frameworks to support academic submission rigor.

Project DNA

Every completed project receives a persistent digital identity preserving the exact configuration of hardware, software, and logic for future reference.

Evolution & Marketplace

Future users fork the repository to improve the physical design or code, while senior cohorts trade unused hardware via a peer-to-peer exchange.

11. RISK & MITIGATION

Risk Factor Mitigation Strategy
Inventory lock-in Start exclusively with highly standardized, high-utilization components.
Hardware damage Implement QR tracking, security deposits, and mandatory inspection.
Academic integrity Strictly prohibit ready-made submissions; focus solely on resources.
Operational complexity Restrict rollout entirely to a controlled college pilot before any expansion.
