### Full Description (Up to 4,000 characters)
**REKORA: From Idea to Prototype. From Prototype to Evolution.**

REKORA is an integrated engineering development and prototyping ecosystem engineered for students, makers, project teams, and campus hardware labs. 

Rather than treating academic engineering projects as disposable single-semester efforts, REKORA connects the entire project lifecycle—lowering the cost of prototyping through hardware rentals, providing guided AI blueprints, enabling campus-based 3D printing fabrication, and preserving technical heritage with persistent Project DNA.

---

### KEY CAPABILITIES:

1. **PROJECT MATCHER & DISCOVERY**
   - Find viable project initiatives matched directly to your exact budget (in ₹ INR), owned components, technical skills, and semester timeline.
   - Transparent preview before unlocking complete project repositories.
   - Explainable match scores highlight feasibility before you spend a single rupee.

2. **AI PROJECT ENGINEER**
   - Diagnostic workspace guiding you through problem clarification, functional constraints, hardware trade-offs, and test planning.
   - Generate structured Project Blueprints with complete Bill of Materials (BOM) and milestones.

3. **COMPONENT SOURCING: BUY, RENT, OR REUSE**
   - Rent costly microcontrollers (ESP32-S3, Raspberry Pi 4), sensor arrays, and motors for just the weeks you need them.
   - Itemized BOM tracker calculates remaining out-of-pocket expenses when you reuse components you already own.
   - Transparent deposit policies: Refundable security deposits are never blended with sales fees.

4. **CAMPUS 3D PRINTING & FABRICATION**
   - Submit STL, STEP, and 3MF files directly to St. Joseph's College campus fab lab.
   - Choose engineering materials: PLA, PETG, ABS, TPU, and Resin with live infill and turnaround estimators.

5. **PEER-TO-PEER (P2P) RESOURCE MARKETPLACE**
   - Exchange unused development kits, mechanical brackets, and tools between senior and junior cohorts within your verified campus community.

6. **STRUCTURED DOCUMENTATION WORKSPACE**
   - Prepare IEEE-compliant project reports, abstracts, methodology, and empirical test logs.
   - Export structured reports to PDF and Markdown with zero hassle.

7. **PROJECT DNA & VERSION EVOLUTION**
   - Every completed project receives a persistent digital identity preserving hardware schematics, CAD revisions, firmware, and validation outcomes.
   - Subsequent student teams can fork existing projects (e.g. v1.0 -> v1.1) to tackle more ambitious, cumulative challenges.

8. **QR LIFECYCLE ASSET TRACKING**
   - Hardware assets tracked via QR codes (e.g. `RK-ESP32-001`) with inspection records and maintenance accountability.

### ACADEMIC INTEGRITY NOTICE
REKORA does NOT sell ready-made project submissions or encourage contract cheating. We provide prototyping infrastructure, hardware access, design assistance, and authentic documentation frameworks. The physical build, software deployment, and academic evaluation remain the authentic responsibility of the student.

### PRIVACY & DATA SAFETY DECLARATION
- **Data Collected**: Name, email address, institutional affiliation (department/college), rough interaction logs for order dispatch and equipment rental accountability.
- **Data Encryption**: All data in transit is encrypted using modern TLS (HTTPS).
- **Payment Safety**: Payments processed via PCI-DSS compliant sandbox/merchant gateway. No debit/credit card data is stored on-device.
- **Account Deletion**: Users may request complete deletion of their account and personal records directly within the in-app Profile Settings or by emailing privacy@rekora.com.

### PERMISSION DECLARATIONS
- `CAMERA`: Used exclusively for scanning hardware QR codes during component pickup, rental returns, and inventory audits.
- `INTERNET`: Required for synchronizing project blueprints, orders, and fabrication job updates.
- `POST_NOTIFICATIONS`: Delivers rental return due-date reminders, fabrication completion notices, and order status alerts.
